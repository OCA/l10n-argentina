VAT Ledger (Libro IVA) for Argentina.

Generates the sale and purchase VAT ledger for a period (invoices
grouped by tax rate, with subtotal per aliquot) and the digital files
required by AFIP/ ARCA's RG 3685 (Régimen de Información de Compras y
Ventas): the header, voucher and aliquot TXT layouts
(`REGDIGITAL_CV_CBTE`, `REGDIGITAL_CV_ALICUOTAS`,
`REGDIGITAL_CV_COMPRAS_IMPORTACIONES`).

Migrated from the OCA 14.0 branch. See the module's ROADMAP for known
gaps in the digital file generation.
